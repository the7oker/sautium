Hidden Orchestra

Ambient Electronic Downtempo Cinematic

Hidden Orchestra is a studio project led by Edinburgh-based composer and producer Joe Acheson. Blending live acoustic and electronic instrumentation — strings, brass, twin drummers — with field recordings collected across Europe, their work lives at the meeting point of modern composition, dub, jazz and cinematic soundtrack.
