Peak meter for gain staging

Reference v1 · 2026-10-07
  • What the needle showsThe true peak per channel at HQPlayer's volume and adaptive gain, before its limiter. Sautium rebuilds it from the source HQPlayer streams on its meter port, 6.25 readings a second, in step with what plays.
  • ScaleClassic VU geometry: deflection follows amplitude, −20…+3 dBTP. A third of the arc covers −3…+3, where gain decisions happen. Terracotta above 0; amber −3…0 only while HQPlayer outputs SDM.
  • BallisticsA peak meter. The needle rises at once and falls 20 dB in 1.5 s. The hold mark and Max keep the highest peak since the sheet opened; Reset or any volume change clears them.
  • Over and LimitedOVER lights while the needle is above 0 dBTP; past +3 the needle rests on its stop and Max gives the figure. Limited is HQPlayer's count of its own limiter acting at the output, from the last volume step.
  • Where it opensThe Meter chip on the Volume row. The connection to HQPlayer's meter port exists only while this sheet is open and the page is visible.
